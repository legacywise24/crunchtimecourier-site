What this covers

  • Shipping coordination for eligible envelopes and parcels
  • Final-mile delivery requests for carriers and businesses
  • Less-than-truckload (LTL) delivery support, reviewed per request
  • Multi-piece and oversized items reviewed for the right method

Who we serve

Businesses that ship regularly, carriers needing a local final-mile hand, and anyone with a shipment too large or too awkward for a standard counter drop-off.

What to expect

  1. Tell us what is moving. Provide the item, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, dimensions, approximate weight, access details and any handling needs.
  2. We review the request. We confirm route feasibility, service eligibility, timing, the appropriate delivery method and pricing.
  3. Approve the service. You review the written scope, price, pickup instructions and responsibilities before the delivery is accepted.
  4. Pickup, handoff & follow-up. Approved work follows the agreed schedule, with delivery or exception follow-up documented as applicable.

Questions

Which carriers do you work with?

Shipping is coordinated through major carriers. The right carrier and method are confirmed when your request is reviewed and priced.

Can you handle freight or LTL?

Less-than-truckload requests are reviewed individually for route, equipment and eligibility before a written price is provided.

What information do you need?

Piece count, dimensions, approximate weight, pickup and delivery addresses, and your needed-by date.
