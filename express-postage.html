Who we serve

Anyone who needs something shipped overnight or express without standing at a counter — businesses, professional offices and individuals.

What to expect

  1. Tell us what is moving. Provide the item, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, dimensions, approximate weight, access details and any handling needs.
  2. We review the request. We confirm route feasibility, service eligibility, timing, the appropriate delivery method and pricing.
  3. Approve the service. You review the written scope, price, pickup instructions and responsibilities before the delivery is accepted.
  4. Pickup, handoff & follow-up. Approved work follows the agreed schedule, with delivery or exception follow-up documented as applicable.

Questions

How does it work?

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, package size and weight, service level and needed-by time — by the request form, by email, or by fax to (929) 273-1833. We price it, and the label comes back by email or text.

How much does it cost?

Each shipment is priced individually after review, and you approve the written price before anything is bought or booked. There are no published label prices or service fees.

Which carriers can I ship with?

USPS through our Stamps.com and Endicia accounts. UPS and FedEx express labels are provided only through authorized reseller channels.
