What this covers

  • Amazon and retail store returns and locker drop-offs
  • Curbside pickups from stores and warehouses
  • Mail and package runs
  • Practical pickup-and-delivery errands for home and office

Who we serve

Busy households and small businesses that need something picked up, dropped off or returned without losing half a day to it.

What to expect

  1. Tell us what is moving. Provide the item, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, dimensions, approximate weight, access details and any handling needs.
  2. We review the request. We confirm route feasibility, service eligibility, timing, the appropriate delivery method and pricing.
  3. Approve the service. You review the written scope, price, pickup instructions and responsibilities before the delivery is accepted.
  4. Pickup, handoff & follow-up. Approved work follows the agreed schedule, with delivery or exception follow-up documented as applicable.

Questions

Can you return packages for me?

Yes — returns and locker drop-offs are standard requests. Tell us what is going back, where it was bought, and where it needs to go.

Do I need a label ready?

If you have a return label, have it ready at pickup. If you are not sure, note it in the request and the team will confirm what is needed.

How is an errand priced?

Per request, after review. You approve the written price before anything is accepted.
