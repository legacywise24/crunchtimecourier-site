What this covers

  • Scheduled recurring pickups and deliveries for business accounts
  • Routes designed around your volume, geography and service requirements
  • Inter-office, office-to-client and facility runs
  • Set up by the team — route pricing is quoted, never published

Who we serve

Businesses, property managers and organizations with regular pickup-and-delivery needs — law and title offices, medical offices, and multi-location operations.

What to expect

  1. Tell us what is moving. Provide the item, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, dimensions, approximate weight, access details and any handling needs.
  2. We review the request. We confirm route feasibility, service eligibility, timing, the appropriate delivery method and pricing.
  3. Approve the service. You review the written scope, price, pickup instructions and responsibilities before the delivery is accepted.
  4. Pickup, handoff & follow-up. Approved work follows the agreed schedule, with delivery or exception follow-up documented as applicable.

Questions

How are recurring routes priced?

Route pricing is quoted individually after the team reviews volume, geography and service requirements. There is no published route rate.

How do we start?

Send the company name, contact, how often you need pickups, and what typically moves. The team designs the route and calls you back.

Can the schedule change week to week?

Schedule changes are handled with the team under your route arrangement — note your flexibility needs in the request.
