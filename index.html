What to expect

  1. Tell us what is moving. Provide the item, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, dimensions, approximate weight, access details and any handling needs.
  2. We review the request. We confirm route feasibility, service eligibility, timing, the appropriate delivery method and pricing.
  3. Approve the service. You review the written scope, price, pickup instructions and responsibilities before the delivery is accepted.
  4. Pickup, handoff & follow-up. Approved work follows the agreed schedule, with delivery or exception follow-up documented as applicable.

Who we serve

Businesses, law, medical and title offices, property managers, event organizers — and consumers with something that has to move. Business-to-business and consumer requests are both welcome; custom requests are evaluated individually so the service matches the route, item and timing.

Service and role clarity

Availability, service area, item eligibility, timing and pricing depend on the specific request. Hazardous, temperature-controlled, regulated, unusually valuable or healthcare-related items require explicit capability and compliance review before acceptance. Passenger, airport and group transportation are separate Legacy Wise services and are not Crunch Time Courier products.
