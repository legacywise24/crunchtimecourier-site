What this covers

  • Documents and envelopes that cannot wait for standard mail
  • Parcels and business materials of all standard sizes
  • Expedited requests reviewed against route and current availability
  • Eligible weekend service, confirmed per request

Who we serve

Businesses, professional offices and individuals with a time-sensitive pickup or delivery. Send the details and the team reviews route, timing and eligibility before quoting.

What to expect

  1. Tell us what is moving. Provide the item, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, dimensions, approximate weight, access details and any handling needs.
  2. We review the request. We confirm route feasibility, service eligibility, timing, the appropriate delivery method and pricing.
  3. Approve the service. You review the written scope, price, pickup instructions and responsibilities before the delivery is accepted.
  4. Pickup, handoff & follow-up. Approved work follows the agreed schedule, with delivery or exception follow-up documented as applicable.

Questions

How fast can you deliver?

Timing depends on the route, the item and current availability. Send the pickup and delivery details and your needed-by time — the team confirms what is possible when it prices the request.

How is it priced?

Every request is priced individually after review. You receive a written price and approve it before the job is accepted — there are no published rate tables.

What do you need from me?

The item description, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, approximate size and weight, and any access or handling details.
