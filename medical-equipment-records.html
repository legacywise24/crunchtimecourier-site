What this covers

  • Medical equipment and device delivery requests, reviewed case by case
  • Medical files and records runs for offices and facilities
  • Requests accepted only after handling and privacy requirements are confirmed
  • No request is accepted until the compliance review is complete

Who we serve

Medical offices, facilities and suppliers whose items carry handling, privacy or training requirements — and who need those requirements confirmed in writing before anything moves.

What to expect

  1. Tell us what is moving. Provide the item, pickup and delivery addresses, time window, dimensions, approximate weight, access details and any handling needs.
  2. We review the request. We confirm route feasibility, service eligibility, timing, the appropriate delivery method and pricing.
  3. Approve the service. You review the written scope, price, pickup instructions and responsibilities before the delivery is accepted.
  4. Pickup, handoff & follow-up. Approved work follows the agreed schedule, with delivery or exception follow-up documented as applicable.

Questions

Do you accept every medical delivery?

No. Healthcare-related items require explicit capability and compliance review before acceptance, and some requests cannot be accepted. Send the details and the team will confirm.

How are records handled?

Records requests are reviewed for privacy and handling requirements before acceptance, and the agreed handling is confirmed in writing.

Can I set up recurring medical pickups?

Recurring routes are set up by the team after review — see the Recurring Business Routes page.
