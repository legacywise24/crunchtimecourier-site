How this service works

Crunch Time Courier does not print in-house. We are your one point of contact for print and branded products: we source your order through vetted print and product suppliers, manage the proof and production, and deliver it where it needs to go. You deal with us — we deal with the suppliers.

  1. Tell us what you need. The product, quantity, sizes, colors, your artwork or wording, and when you need it. No artwork yet? Tell us — design help can be quoted as part of the job.
  2. We source it and quote it in writing. One written quote covering the item, production and delivery. There are no published prices — every job is quoted per request.
  3. You approve the price and the proof. Nothing goes into production until you have approved both, in writing.
  4. Production and delivery. The supplier produces your order; where the delivery is local, Crunch Time delivers it. Out-of-area orders ship to you.

Questions

Do you print in-house?

No. Crunch Time Courier is a sourcing and coordination service: your order is produced by vetted print and product suppliers we work with, and we manage the quote, proof, production and delivery for you. One point of contact, start to finish.

How is print work priced?

There are no published print prices. Send what you need and we source it and send one written quote covering the item, quantity, production and delivery. Nothing is produced until you approve the quote and the proof.

Do I see a proof before my order is produced?

Yes — always. You approve a proof of your artwork, wording, sizes and colors in writing before anything goes into production. Changes after proof approval can mean a new quote.

How long does an order take?

Turnaround depends on the product, quantity and the producing supplier. Your written quote states the production and delivery timing before you commit, and rush options are quoted when they exist.

What is the difference between this and Print, Fax & Deliver?

Print, Fax & Deliver is for documents — printing pages, faxing and delivering paperwork. This page is for produced goods: decals, cards, uniforms, imprinted marketing items and labels that are manufactured to order by a supplier.
